Xylon Returns

Video Game Development

Click the photo above to play the beta!
Xylon Returns is the game I created while I was in school for a Game Dev class that I took in my final semester. I worked with a partner who made a vast majority of the art assets and I was the creative lead in the general aesthetics as well the coder for the game. I put a lot of time and effort into this game within the two month period that we had to complete this game. I actually put more time into this project then my final exhibition piece to commemmorate my time at SJSU. This was definitely a project that I am really proud of and it has sparked an interest for me to potentially have a career in Game Development.
